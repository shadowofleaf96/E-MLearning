Frequently Asked Questions

How do streaks work?

expand_more

Streaks track your daily learning consistency. Complete at least one lesson or coding challenge every 24 hours to keep your streak alive. Missing a day resets your streak to zero, unless you use a Streak Freeze.

Are certificates accredited?

expand_more

Our professional certificates are recognized by top tech companies and industry leaders. While they are not traditional university degrees, they serve as verifiable proof of your skill mastery in specific technical domains.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

expand_more

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. You will continue to have access to your paid features until the end of your current billing period.
